Bugfix
   * Fix the TLS 1.2 ECDHE premaster secret buffer being sized from the
     built-in ECP module, which left it a single byte when every curve
     comes from a driver or a separate PSA Crypto implementation and PSK
     key exchanges are disabled. It is now sized from the curves PSA
     Crypto is configured with, which also keeps mbedtls/ssl.h building
     when psa/crypto_struct.h is replaced through
     MBEDTLS_PSA_CRYPTO_STRUCT_FILE.
